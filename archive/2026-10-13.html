    How to play

    The clue is a short phrase. Find a synonym for each of its two main words, so that swapping their opening sounds (a spoonerism) makes a different, common phrase.

    Enter that final phrase. The little words (and, of, the…) are filled in for you. Spelling counts. Take as many guesses as you need: like golf, fewer is better.

    Each puzzle has a par, and a spoon to match: Teaspoon is par 1, Tablespoon 2, Scoop 3–4, Ladle 5–7 and Dipper 8 or more.

    Stuck? Some puzzles offer a hint after your first guess. It doesn't count against you.
